# Nexuses

A nexus folds whole chambers into one machine. The cores of the chambers stand on pedestals around the nexus, and when it forms each core tears a rift open and its chamber comes back through it, shrunk to a working miniature.

A nexus still needs a [wormhole](Distortion-Energy) linked to a Distortion Chamber, and takes the Parallel upgrade.

## Alchemical Nexus

The whole alchemical cycle in one machine: it breaks down, transmutes, condenses and rebuilds on its own, and needs no molds.

- **Heart:** the Nexus Heart, grown around a nether star from diamond, emerald, gold and netherite compounds
- **Structure:** four cores on pedestals around the Alchemical Nexus (destructuration, transmutation, condensator and reconstruction)
- **Use:** choose a target, feed it ores and water
- **Notes:** choosing a new target starts it over, but it keeps its fluids. Each stage costs what its own machine would. Compounds between stages wait in a store of 256. Any of the four cores opens the nexus

## Experience Nexus

Mob drops in, liquid experience out. It breaks down mob drops or essences, condenses its own fluid from water, and renders the experience.

- **Heart:** the Soul Heart, grown around a nether star with echo shards and the essences of ghast tears, shulkers, blazes and phantoms
- **Structure:** a destructuration, a condensator and a rendering core on pedestals, with a cauldron on the floor in front that shows the experience. Its front turns to whoever places it
- **Taking the experience:** the same ways as the Rendering Cauldron: bucket or pipe, its window, Experience Taps on any of its blocks, or one bound Experience Siphon

## Settings

`config/alchymastery-client.toml`, section `nexusFormation`, sets how the folding plays: whether it plays at all, the timing of each step, the size of the rifts, how see-through the hidden chambers are, the particles and the sounds.
