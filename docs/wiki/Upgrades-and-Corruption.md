# Upgrades and Corruption

Every machine has an upgrade tab on the right of its screen, with one slot per kind of upgrade. One upgrade per slot. When a core is broken, its upgrades stay on the dropped core and come back when it is placed again.

## Speed

| Tier | Speed | Energy per task | Distortion matrix |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | x1.5 | +10% | burns x1.5 faster, 13% less DE per stone |
| II | x2 | +20% | burns x2 faster, 23% less DE per stone |
| III | x3 | +30% | burns x3 faster, 33% less DE per stone |
| IV | x4 | +40% | burns x4 faster, 43% less DE per stone |

## Productivity

More from the same input. Each machine gains something different.

| Tier | Byproduct chance | Transmutation loss | Reconstruction fluid | Condensator fluid and matrix DE | Experience |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | 10% | 15% | -15% | +25% | +15% |
| II | 15% | 10% | -30% | +50% | +30% |
| III | 20% | 7% | -45% | +75% | +45% |
| IV | 25% | 5% | -60% | +100% | +60% |

## Efficiency

| Tier | Energy per task | Distortion matrix |
|---|---|---|
| I | -20% | one stone in seven burns without being used up |
| II | -40% | nearly one stone in three |
| III | -60% | almost half the stones |

Speed and Efficiency balance each other: Speed IV with Efficiency III runs four times faster for about half the energy.

## Parallel

Nexuses only. Every stage of the nexus works at the pace of its fastest stage, so no stage waits on another. Speed still applies on top.

## Corruption

Upgrades push the void into a machine, and it shows. Rifts tear open across its blocks with the End glimmering through, in the colours of the installed upgrades. They close and open elsewhere. The more upgrade tiers installed, the more rifts. Corruption is only visual.
