# Distortion Energy

Distortion energy (DE) powers every machine. It is made in the Distortion Chamber and carried to the other machines through wormholes.

## The Distortion Chamber

The core is the **Distortion Matrix**. Build the chamber around it: stone bricks and glass for the walls, glowstone and campfires for light, amethyst above and below the matrix, and a Distortion Crystal on the roof. When it forms, the amethyst hardens into diamond and the campfires burn blue.

The matrix holds up to 100,000 DE, but only while its chamber stands.

### Fuel

| Fuel | DE |
|---|---|
| Lapis lazuli | 100 |
| Block of lapis | 900 |

Fuel burns at 5 DE per tick, so a lapis block burns for 9 seconds. Drop the fuel in through the chamber's window, or feed it with a hopper. The stones float around the matrix until their turn. The fuel list can be changed in the config, see [Configuration and Datapacks](Configuration-and-Datapacks).

You can read the stored energy in the matrix's window, or on its walls when you look at them closely.

## The Distortion Crystal

The crystal sits on the chamber's roof and gathers the energy. It spins faster while the matrix burns. No chamber forms without one.

## Wormholes

Every machine other than the Distortion Chamber needs a **Wormhole** in its structure (usually on its roof). A wormhole linked to a chamber draws energy from it up to 124 blocks away, in the same dimension.

- Unlinked, a wormhole is a sealed shell of obsidian. Linked, its cage turns slowly, and its eye opens while the machine draws energy.
- To link: use the **Wormhole Attuner** on the chamber (its core or any frame block), then on the machine (core or frame). Light arcs between them while they are bound.
- To clear the attuner, sneak and use it in the air.

A machine without a linked wormhole does not work.
